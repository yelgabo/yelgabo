# PhotoBomb marketing image

Create a wide portfolio image that shows the app's purpose and its actual iPhone
interface. Use a simulator screen containing verified synthetic sample scans.
Keep the app's controls and layout intact. Use the app's blue and orange identity
in a simple product composition with printed photos beside the phone.

The final image will replace the icon on the homepage card and appear as the
lead figure on the PhotoBomb project page. Keep the icon as the small app mark.

## Generation prompt

Use case: ads-marketing. Create a polished wide 16:10 product marketing image for
PhotoBomb, an iPhone app for extracting several printed photos from one shot.

Reference 1 is an authentic screenshot of the app in Xcode Device Hub. Use only
the phone screen, ignoring all Mac window chrome, sidebar, cursor, and simulator
controls. Preserve the app's white Sample scans gallery, its colorful sample
thumbnails, "20 photos from 2 shots", shot groups, and bottom controls. Keep its
layout and labels faithful. Reference 2 is the app icon, for brand colors only.

Compose a large realistic black iPhone at a slight angle on the right, with the
real app gallery clearly visible. Beside it, arrange a small stack of printed
abstract landscape pictures matching the colorful sample images on the screen.
Use an off-white tactile tabletop, a restrained cobalt-blue paper backdrop, and
a small orange accent drawn from the icon. Soft daylight and natural shadows.
Keep the phone almost front-facing so the UI stays legible. Make the phone and
prints fill the frame with enough breathing room for a portfolio card crop.

The only marketing text is "PhotoBomb", set cleanly near the upper left. Add no
other slogans, badges, claims, people, watermarks, or invented interface elements.
Output a finished landscape bitmap around 1600 by 1000 pixels.

Mode: built-in ImageGen. The UI reference uses the existing simulator's synthetic
test session, copied into its otherwise empty default library and titled Sample
scans. Its two originals were verified against the project's synthetic fixtures.

Saved asset: `assets/projects/photobomb-marketing.png`.
